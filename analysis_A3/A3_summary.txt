INTERPRETATION:
  Test-retest: REMARKABLY STABLE across all subgroups (~0.23). UA noise does NOT
  inflate test-retest -- collisions add unrelated scores, which should DECREASE r.
  The fact that r stays the same suggests collisions are rare among 2-attempt users.

  Practice effect: EMERGES in specific UAs (slope=+0.22/attempt, p=0.012).
  The full-sample null result (p=0.118) was a COLLISION ARTIFACT: generic UAs with
  many "attempts" are actually different people, washing out real practice gains.
  When restricted to identifiable individuals, there IS a small practice effect.

Suspicious criteria: high hour entropy (activity spread across all 24h),

Records in genuine heavy-user UAs: 1,754

CONFIDENCE ASSESSMENT
---------------------
High confidence (single person):  ~15% of records
  - Specific Android models + unique browser version combos
  - iPhones via Facebook in-app browser (FBDV tag)
  
Moderate confidence:              ~26% of records  
  - iPhones with specific iOS version (limited collision risk since 
    iOS version space is large, but not impossible)

Low confidence (possible collision): ~59% of records
  - Desktop browsers (OS + browser version is NOT person-specific)
  - Generic Android ("K" model = privacy-masked Android 13+)
  - Very heavy users almost always fall here

IMPLICATIONS FOR B1 FINDINGS:
  1. Test-retest r=0.231 is ROBUST -- not inflated by collisions
  2. "No practice effect" was WRONG for real individuals -- specific UAs show
     slope=+0.22/attempt (p=0.012). Collisions masked this in the full sample.
  3. Personal best at attempt 2-3 is consistent with the small positive practice slope.
  4. Heavy users (>=50 att) are mostly multi-person collisions, not single individuals.
